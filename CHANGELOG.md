# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First public release.

- Consolidated copy `Title (annotated).epub` with highlights and underlines: KOReader's
  style or a chosen one (background, solid, dashed, wavy), per color, with an e-ink gray.
- Reader's notes in the text, or as pop-up notes with a `[1]` reference and a
  "Reader's notes" page.
- Personal index by themes (`#theme`, `#theme/sub-theme`), also in the table of contents
  (EPUB 3 nav and EPUB 2 NCX), optionally with sub-themes.
- Reading note on the title page.
- Analysis of annotations without changing anything, with the reason for each skipped one.
- Passages across several paragraphs of the same chapter.
- English interface, Italian translation.
- Requires KOReader 2024.07 or later. EPUB 2 and 3 without DRM.
